Snowflake No.02

Hexagonal Ice Crystal Growth Line Art · Snowflake

Each cell holds some water vapor. Cells next to the ice gain a little vapor every step; the rest share it with their neighbours. Where vapor arrives fastest, the crystal reaches out, and the arms branch. The six-fold symmetry comes from the hexagonal lattice, not from copying.

Parameters

  • diffusion α = 1.01
  • background vapor β = 0.427
  • vapor supply γ = 0.00012
  • lattice radius = 149 cells
  • growth rings = 6
